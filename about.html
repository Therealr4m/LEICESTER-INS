About Leicester Loft Insulation

Loft insulation, top-ups, boarding and removal for residential loft spaces.

Start with the work your home needs

Some lofts need a complete installation. Others need an extra layer of insulation or a small boarded area for storage. The right starting point is to look at what is already there and how you want to use the space.

Before any work is agreed, the condition of the loft, ventilation, access and any visible problems should be considered. If something needs specialist advice or repair, that should be raised before proceeding.

A clear plan before work starts

Ask for a written scope that sets out the proposed material, preparation, work area and price. Boarding, removal and access changes should be listed where they are part of the job.

For an initial enquiry, your postcode and a short description of the loft are useful. Mention any existing insulation, storage boards or damp concerns.
