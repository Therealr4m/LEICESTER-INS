Loft Insulation Installation in Leicester

New loft insulation for homes with little or no existing insulation.

When a new installation makes sense

If your loft has little insulation, or has been cleared ready for new material, a full installation may be the right starting point. The first step is to understand the space: how it is used, how it is ventilated and whether any repairs are needed.

A standard cold-loft installation insulates at ceiling level, leaving the roof space above unheated. A loft conversion needs a different design. Tell us if the space is a room, or if you plan to convert it later.

What needs checking first?

The loft should be checked for damp, roof leaks and signs of damage before insulation is laid. Ventilation at the eaves must remain clear. Access, pipework, water tanks and electrical fittings also need to be considered.

Insulation should not simply be laid over everything in the loft. Cables and recessed lights may need particular clearances or advice from an electrician. Any problems found should be discussed before the scope of work is agreed.

Planning the work

A quote should explain what material is proposed, where it will be fitted, what preparation is included and how access will be handled. If you need storage, plan the boarding at the same time so insulation is not compressed.

Please include your postcode, approximate loft size and any photos you already have when making an enquiry. Only take photos from a safe, accessible position; there is no need to enter a loft just to request information.

Common Questions

Can insulation be fitted in every loft?

The right method depends on the roof type, existing condition and how the space is used. A converted or occupied loft needs different treatment from an unheated roof space.

Will insulation fix a damp loft?

No. The cause of damp needs to be identified and dealt with. Roof leaks, condensation and ventilation issues should be assessed before fitting insulation.
