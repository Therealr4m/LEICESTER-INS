Loft Insulation Top-Ups in Leicester

Extra insulation where existing material is suitable to keep.

Does your loft need a top-up?

Some lofts already have insulation but the coverage is uneven or the depth is limited. A top-up can be an option where the existing material is dry, in reasonable condition and suitable to retain.

The current insulation needs to be inspected before adding more. Gaps, compressed sections and material moved during other work can all affect the coverage. The answer is not always to add the same depth across the whole space.

Keep the useful material, address the problems

Existing insulation does not always need to be removed. Keeping suitable material can avoid unnecessary work. Damaged or contaminated sections, however, may require a separate plan.

A top-up should account for ventilation and the position of cables, pipes and fittings. If boards are already fitted tightly over insulation, the boarding arrangement may need changing before an extra layer can be added.

What to include in your enquiry

Tell us roughly when the loft was last insulated, whether you use it for storage and whether you have noticed damp or cold spots. These details help establish what needs checking.

The final specification should follow an assessment of your loft. Ask for the material, proposed coverage, preparation and any changes to boarding to be set out in the quote.

Common Questions

Can new material go over old insulation?

In many cases it can, if the old material is dry, suitable and in good condition. Its condition must be checked first.

Can I lay boards straight over a top-up?

Insulation should not be squashed beneath boards. A suitable raised boarding arrangement may be needed, subject to the loft’s structure and access.
