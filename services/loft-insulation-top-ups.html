Loft Insulation top ups in Leicester lofts

Our dedicated loft insulation team at Eco Leicester Insulation adds modern insulation materials in lofts where existing insulation may require an additional top-up to reach the UK recommended total depth of 270mm for mineral wool insulation.

A loft insulation top-up allows us to add to what’s already there, increasing thermal depth and performance without you needing a full loft insulation fitting from scratch.

What we check before a top-up

Existing insulation needs to be dry and suitable to keep. We consider its condition, coverage and depth alongside ventilation, access and any boarding already in place.

If material is damp, damaged or contaminated, that needs to be addressed before more insulation is added.

Adding depth without compressing insulation

The aim is even coverage at the specified depth. Insulation should not be compressed under storage boards, and necessary ventilation routes must remain clear.

270mm is a mineral wool recommendation. The required thickness is different for other insulation materials, including rigid PIR boards.

Loft insulation top-up questions

Can you add new insulation over old material?

Often, if the existing insulation is dry, suitable and in good condition. Its condition and the loft’s ventilation should be checked first.

Does 270mm apply to every insulation material?

No. The 270mm recommendation is for mineral wool loft insulation. Other materials need a suitable specification for the system and property.

Will I need a full new fitting?

Not always. A top-up may be suitable if the insulation already in place can be retained.
