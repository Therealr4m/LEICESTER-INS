Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers before you plan work in your loft.

Can insulation be fitted in every loft?

The right method depends on the roof type, existing condition and how the space is used. A converted or occupied loft needs different treatment from an unheated roof space.

Will insulation fix a damp loft?

No. The cause of damp needs to be identified and dealt with. Roof leaks, condensation and ventilation issues should be assessed before fitting insulation.

Can new material go over old insulation?

In many cases it can, if the old material is dry, suitable and in good condition. Its condition must be checked first.

Can I lay boards straight over a top-up?

Insulation should not be squashed beneath boards. A suitable raised boarding arrangement may be needed, subject to the loft’s structure and access.

Does boarding turn a loft into a bedroom?

No. Creating a habitable room involves different structural, access, fire safety and building requirements. Storage boarding is a separate service.

Does the entire loft need boarding?

Not necessarily. A smaller area or access route may meet your needs. The layout should reflect the intended use and the structure.

Should all old insulation be removed?

No. Age alone does not establish whether removal is necessary. Dry, suitable material in good condition may be retained.

Can replacement insulation be included?

Removal and replacement can be discussed together. Any underlying damp, damage or contamination needs addressing before the new installation.
