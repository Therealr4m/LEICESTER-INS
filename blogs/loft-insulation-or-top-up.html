New Insulation or a Top-Up?

How to decide what your existing loft insulation needs.

Start with what is already there

A loft with little or no insulation needs a different plan from one with a dry, even layer already fitted. Before choosing a service, check what you know about the material and whether it has been disturbed by storage or other work.

When a top-up may be suitable

Existing insulation may be retained if it is dry, suitable and in good condition. Gaps, compressed areas and boarding need consideration before more material is added. The final approach should follow an assessment of the loft.

When to ask about removal

Damage, damp or contamination can change the plan. Replacing insulation without addressing the cause may leave the same problem in the new material. If you do not recognise the insulation, do not disturb it just to identify it.

What to send with an enquiry

Include your postcode, what you know about the existing insulation and whether the loft is used for storage. Share existing photos if you have them, but do not enter a loft just to take pictures.
