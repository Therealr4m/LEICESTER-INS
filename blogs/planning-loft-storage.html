Planning Storage in Your Loft

What to consider before adding loft boarding.

Decide how much space you need

A small boarded area near the hatch may be enough for occasional storage. Think about which items you want to store, their weight and how often you need to reach them. Boarding the entire loft is not always necessary.

Allow room for insulation

Boards should not compress insulation. A suitable raised system may provide space underneath, subject to the loft structure and access. Plan the insulation and boarding together rather than treating them as separate jobs.

Check access and ventilation

The hatch, ladder and route through the loft affect how usable a storage area will be. Ventilation routes must stay clear. Cables, pipes and fittings also need to be considered before the layout is agreed.

Agree the scope in writing

Ask the quote to identify the area to be boarded, the proposed system and any changes to access. Storage boarding does not turn a loft into a habitable room. If you want a room conversion, that requires a different assessment.
